1. Continuous Zero-Trust Engine
Multi-Factor Verification

Eliminates implicit trust in RFID tags. Every supply chain package is dynamically re-verified at each checkpoint using a weighted Trust Score (0–100) evaluating 8 physical and contextual vectors.

2. Package Tamper Rupture Sensing
< 50μs Hardware Trigger

A conductive wire seal is taped across the box opening. If sliced or opened in transit, the GPIO 4 circuit is broken, immediately penalizing the trust score by 80 points and forcing an instant QUARANTINE lockdown.

3. Dynamic Route Topology Guard
Sequential Node Tracking

Enforces strict supply chain progression across all facilities (Manufacturer → Warehouse → Distributor → Retailer). Bypassing inspection hubs or backwards cargo movement is instantly detected.

Replay & Duplicate Defense

Monitors concurrent tag usage across distant nodes. Enforces physical transit velocity bounds ($t \ge \text{min\_time}$) to immediately flag replayed scan tokens and duplicate cloned RFID tags.
